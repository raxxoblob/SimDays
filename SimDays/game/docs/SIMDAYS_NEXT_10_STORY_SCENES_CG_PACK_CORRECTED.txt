first Grounds introduction, or other onboarding scenes that already exist.

foundation.

relationship/career/world state.

01A — Zoe waiting at the water

Create a 16:9 horizontal 2D visual-novel CG designed for 1920x1080 presentation.

VISUAL STYLE:
Use the supplied city reference image as the rendering-language target, not as a literal location reference. Aim for a polished modern Japanese visual-novel / anime-film background style: crisp controlled linework, strong believable architectural perspective, richly observed but simplified urban detail, clear readable shapes, lush grouped foliage where appropriate, luminous skies, soft atmospheric depth, painterly cel-style shading, restrained texture, natural color relationships and low-to-medium contrast on characters.

Characters must match their established reference images exactly in face, hairstyle, body proportions and approved clothing. Keep adult anime/VN proportions grounded and believable. Clothing should look matte, with broad soft shadows and restrained highlights. Integrate characters naturally into the environment rather than making them look pasted over a background.

AVOID:
photorealism, pseudo-3D rendering, glossy AI-looking clothes, plastic skin, over-sharpened fabric folds, exaggerated rim light, neon cyberpunk color treatment, orange/teal grading, huge bloom, fashion-photo posing, characters staring at the viewer without narrative reason, exaggerated blush, overly sexualized body language, impossible hands, duplicated fingers, warped gym equipment, unreadable fake text dominating the composition.

SCENE:
Night beach. Zoe is already near the shoreline when MC arrives after her deliberate invitation. The beach is familiar from earlier Zoe content, but the mood is different because this time she asked MC to come specifically. Deep blue sky, soft distant city lights, subtle reflections on wet sand. Zoe notices MC approaching and turns toward him. No sketchbook is needed unless the current scene continuity explicitly includes it.

COMPOSITION / CAMERA:
Wide cinematic establishing shot. MC appears from the foreground/rear three-quarter; Zoe is mid-distance near the water. Use the shoreline as a leading line toward the city.

Preserve all established character identity, wardrobe and location continuity from the current SimDays references.
