## Design principles

- **Discovery, not a menu.** You meet people by being in their place at the right time — no master NPC list shown to the player.
- **Each character has a "where + when".** A schedule (which district, which days/hours) so the world feels alive and you plan around it.
- **Mixed cast.** Male and female, romance *and* friendship *and* rivalry/mentor — not everyone is a date.
- **Hobbies/interests drive content.** Each person has 2–3 interests → gift ideas, conversation topics that land, and date/hangout spots they like.
- **Two meters:** Affection (0–100) and Trust (0–100), separate. Scenes unlock at thresholds + stat checks (CHR/APP/INT…).
- **Relationship types:** Friend · Romance · Rival→Friend · Mentor · Business partner · Antagonist arc.

### Visual status hierarchy
Characters' attractiveness and polish reflect their world — not everyone is conventionally beautiful, and that's intentional:
- **Early-game / open-world characters** (park, café, home block): average-looking, distinctive personality. A barista looks like a real barista. Makes the world feel grounded.
- **Mid-tier characters** (warehouse crew, gym regulars): fit/rough/practical looks matching their context.
- **High-status characters** (corporate managers, VIP contacts, doctors): polished, well-dressed, conventionally attractive. The visual reward for progressing.
- **Exception:** some high-status characters are deliberately plain/ugly (power without looks — e.g. a gruff warehouse boss, a balding corporate shark). Makes the attractive ones feel earned.

### Progression-locked characters
Some characters are **only met once you reach a certain rank or status** — they don't exist to the player until the gate opens:
- **Corporate job rank 3+** → meet a senior manager / exec in the break room
- **Money ≥ $5000 or VIP invite** → airport lounge / rooftop bar / club, characters there are high-status
- **Gym Trainer tier** → Victor the owner opens up; Becca's deeper arc
- **Late-night / nightlife** (hour ≥ 22) → some bar characters only appear at specific times
- Gate is soft: you *could* have walked past them before, but didn't have access. No "quest marker" — discovery.

## Relationship arc structure (proposed)

1. **Stranger** → first meeting (scripted, location-gated).
2. **Acquaintance** (Affection 1–25): small talk, learn 1 interest. Journal entry appears.
3. **Friend** (25–50): hangouts unlock, gift reactions, a personal mini-quest.
4. **Close** (50–75): their main storyline beat; a choice that moves Trust a lot.
5. **Romance/Partner OR Best friend** (75–100): confession/commitment scene, gated by stat (e.g., CHR/APP) + Trust.
6. **Endings:** partner · best friend · falling out · neutral drift.

Each interest a character likes → +bonus when talked about or gifted; disliked topics → penalty. (Drives the "get to know them" loop.)
