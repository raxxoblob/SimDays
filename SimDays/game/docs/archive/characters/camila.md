## Canon Identity

Age: 29
Pronouns: she/her
Role: bartender and informal shift leader at Static
Relationship scope: full friendship and potential romance

## Core Personality

Camila is socially skilled because of her work, but this must not be confused with immediate personal openness.

She is:

- observant,
- quick-witted,
- calm during conflict,
- protective of staff and regular customers,
- privately cautious,
- unimpressed by status without behaviour to support it.

Primary strength: she reads social situations quickly.

Primary flaw: she sometimes decides what a person is like before giving them enough time to prove otherwise.

## Voice and Dialogue Rules

- concise and conversational,
- dry humour,
- rarely explains a joke,
- asks direct questions,
- does not become flirtatious merely because MC is polite,
- does not deliver long emotional monologues at work,
- notices behaviour toward workers and strangers.

## Relationship Progression

Initial state: Liking 0, Trust 0.

**Before formal introduction:** Camila may serve MC and appear in ambient scenes, but she is not yet added as a full contact.

**Formal introduction unlock:** after two completed visits to Static, OR after the existing social-status value reaches the later approved threshold. The exact numeric threshold is not yet canon.

**Stage 1:** recognises MC as a returning customer.

**Stage 2:** begins speaking personally rather than only professionally.

**Stage 3:** friendship or romance direction may develop through later approved events.

## Liking and Trust Behavior

Liking increases from:
- treating staff respectfully,
- handling awkward situations without entitlement,
- remembering small details,
- humour that does not humiliate another person.

Liking decreases from:
- treating workers as socially invisible,
- entitlement,
- aggressive flirting,
- public disrespect.

Trust increases from:
- consistency,
- discretion,
- defending someone without performing for attention.

Trust decreases from:
- lying after being directly asked,
- using private information socially,
- escalating conflict for entertainment.

## Jealousy and Boundaries

Jealousy level: medium after relationship Stage 2.

Not triggered by:
- ordinary Talk,
- work conversations,
- meeting new people,
- casual friendships.

Possible later triggers:
- witnessed flirtation,
- witnessed kiss,
- exclusivity being implied and then contradicted,
- repeated attention to another romantic interest while avoiding Camila.

Jealousy must produce dialogue and choices before automatic penalties.

Jealousy cannot trigger before Stage 2.
