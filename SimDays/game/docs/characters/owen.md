## Core Personality

Owen is:

- patient,
- practical,
- observant,
- understated,
- comfortable with silence,
- mildly amused by people who complicate simple problems.

Primary strength: he listens before offering advice.

Primary flaw: he can avoid necessary confrontation by making a joke and letting the moment pass.

## Voice and Dialogue Rules

- plain language,
- dry humour,
- short stories about work and the city,
- rarely dramatic,
- does not speak like a philosopher,
- never behaves as an exposition machine,
- advice comes from lived experience rather than authority.
