Sloane is:

- polished,
- selective,
- socially strategic,
- composed,

- attentive to reputation,

## Liking and Trust Behavior

Liking increases from:
- social awareness,
- discretion,
- confidence without boasting,
- recognising when not to dominate a conversation.

Liking decreases from:
- obvious name-dropping,
- public awkwardness created for attention,
- trying to impress her with money alone.

Trust increases from:
- discretion,
- professional competence,
- keeping sensitive information private.

Trust decreases from:
- public embarrassment,
- leaking information,
- pretending to know people or facts MC does not know.

## Jealousy and Boundaries

Jealousy level: low.

She is more sensitive to:
- public disrespect,
- being socially undermined,
- contradictions between private and public behaviour.

Ordinary conversations with other NPCs do not trigger jealousy.
